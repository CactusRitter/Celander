## 2.0.0
- New design shared with Cemail and Cenotes: six designs to choose from (Glass, Modern, AMOLED, Retro 98, Paper, Brutalist), an accent colour (eight, or any hue) and the glass transparency, all in Settings > Appearance.
- Glass V2: a sharper blur, a transparency curve that keeps text readable, and shadows that stay outside the glass.
- Settings are now a short tree of sections, like in the other apps.
- Updates now download inside the app, with a progress bar. Celander looks on GitHub once or twice a day.
- Accounts like in Cemail: connect Google or Microsoft (Outlook.com, Hotmail, Microsoft 365) in Settings > Accounts and Celander keeps their calendars here, also offline. Changes you make are sent to the account within seconds; changes made elsewhere arrive every minute while the app is open and every 15 minutes in the background.
- Google Tasks can be switched on for each Google account. The phone's own calendar is no longer read.
- Subscribe to a calendar published as an .ics link (read only), and import an .ics file into this device.
- Backup in one .zip with check boxes: settings, and the calendars, events and tasks of this device. Accounts are not in the backup: sign in again.
- Delete all data now also disconnects your accounts. What is stored at Google or Microsoft stays there.
- Changing one occurrence of a repeating event back to the original now reaches the account too.
