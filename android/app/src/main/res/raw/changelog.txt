## 2.4.3
- Accounts and network now live in the shared Ce module (CeKit): no visible changes. You stay signed in.

## 2.4.2
- Fixed: Google birthdays already saved always disappear when you turn on contact birthdays, even if a new account's long sync finishes later (duplicates could remain before).
- Fixed: the Google account photo did not show on some phones; a failed attempt is now retried after 6 hours and the reason appears in Diagnostics.

## 2.4.1
- Fixed: turning on contact birthdays no longer leaves duplicates; the birthdays Google had already saved disappear when you enable it.

## 2.4.0
- Contact birthdays (Settings → Accounts → Google account, off by default): shown in their own read-only "Birthdays" calendar in the drawer, views, search and widgets. It asks for contacts permission separately.
- Drawer: the calendar list only shows the calendars enabled in Accounts → Manage calendars; disabled ones disappear from the drawer, views, search and default pickers (their reminders still arrive).
- Settings: Data is no longer cut off at the bottom; the push switch is gone (push is always on); removing an account or deleting all data stops its notifications.
- Fixed: "What's new" in Settings → Updates was empty for the current version.

## 2.3.0
- Backup now uses the common Ce code (same .zip format).
- Updated shared design module (CeKit).

## 2.2.0
- Shared design code with the other Ce apps (new common module): dialogs, menus, buttons, chips and switches now follow the chosen design everywhere (Glass, Modern, AMOLED, Retro 98, Paper, Brutalist). Menus and notices are never see-through enough to mix with the text below.
- Settings → Appearance, Updates and Diagnostics are now the same in every Ce app.
- Event editor: switches, buttons and the Event/Task selector follow Retro 98 and the other designs.
- Accent colours are now called Ochre and Rust, like in the other apps.

## Unreleased
- Month view: the previous month no longer peeks in at the left edge next to the week numbers. The selected day follows the look (corners and outline width of each design), and the empty day panel no longer has the extra button at the bottom.

## 2.1.2
- New app icon in the style of Cemail and Cenotes: a calendar on a light background, also as a themed icon.
- New Diagnostics page in Settings (as in Cemail): an activity log of account sign-ins, syncs and updates, plus the last unexpected error, that you can share or copy. It never holds event titles, addresses or tokens.

## 2.1.1
- Fixed: tapping Download in the update notice closed the app. Celander now declares the permission Android needs to hand the new version to its installer.

## 2.1.0
- Accounts look like Cemail's: your picture from Google or Microsoft inside a ring in the account's colour, and one "Add" button that opens "Add an account" with a card for Google, Microsoft and the .ics link.
- The dialog to subscribe to a calendar link now uses the same glass as the other dialogs, and dialogs no longer turn the screen behind them fully black.

## 2.0.0
- New design shared with Cemail and Cenotes: six designs to choose from (Glass, Modern, AMOLED, Retro 98, Paper, Brutalist), an accent colour (eight, or any hue) and the glass transparency, all in Settings > Appearance.
- Glass V2: a sharper blur, a transparency curve that keeps text readable, and shadows that stay outside the glass.
- Settings are now a short tree of sections, like in the other apps.
- Updates now download inside the app, with a progress bar. Celander looks on GitHub once or twice a day.
- Accounts like in Cemail: connect Google or Microsoft (Outlook.com, Hotmail, Microsoft 365) in Settings > Accounts and Celander keeps their calendars here, also offline. Changes you make are sent to the account within seconds; changes made elsewhere arrive every minute while the app is open and every 15 minutes in the background.
- Google Tasks can be switched on for each Google account. The phone's own calendar is no longer read.
- Subscribe to a calendar published as an .ics link (read only), and import an .ics file into this device.
- Backup in one .zip with check boxes: settings, and the calendars, events and tasks of this device. Accounts are not in the backup: sign in again.
- Delete all data now also disconnects your accounts. What is stored at Google or Microsoft stays there.
- Changing one occurrence of a repeating event back to the original now reaches the account too.
- One background under all screens: opening or closing Settings no longer slides the background or shifts its tone during the transition.
- Side menu (☰) like Cemail and Cenotes: views, calendars with a visibility box (grouped by account), Manage calendars, Accounts and Settings. The ☰ replaces the Settings button in the top bar.
- Instant calendar updates (push) with Google and Microsoft, with a switch in Settings › Calendar (off: checked every minute while the app is open and every 15 minutes otherwise; same without Play Services). Only active when the app ships with the service keys.
